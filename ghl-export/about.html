About Talbott Investments

Meet the person behind the investment process.

Our Firm

Talbott Investments acquires and manages value add multifamily communities in growing markets, alongside experienced operating partners. We help busy professionals build passive income through real assets.

Our work centers on three things: disciplined acquisitions, institutional quality operating partners, and clear investor communication.

Leadership

Founder and Managing Partner

Stephen Predmore

Stephen Predmore is the Managing Partner of Talbott Investments, where he leads investment selection, due diligence, and capital formation. Since 2019, he has invested in multifamily, hospitality, and self-storage assets as both an active and passive investor.

Stephen oversees every stage of the investment process: evaluating markets, screening opportunities against Talbott’s acquisition criteria, and underwriting each deal with conservative assumptions and downside scenarios. He partners exclusively with established operating firms that have the scale, systems, and track record to execute the business plan.

He also leads capital formation and investor relations, working directly with investors from the first conversation through ongoing reporting and distributions. Stephen holds a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from the University at Buffalo and is a licensed Professional Engineer, which shapes his disciplined, data driven approach to evaluating risk.

Outside of Talbott, Stephen volunteers in construction with Habitat for Humanity and is an Eagle Scout. He grew up in Rochester, New York, the youngest of seven children, and now lives in Maryland with his wife.

Advisor · Cramlet Capital

Yohannes Cramlet

Yohannes Cramlet is the Founder and Managing Partner of Cramlet Capital, where he leads every investment decision, overseeing acquisitions, underwriting, operator selection, asset management, and capital formation. He brings more than 25 years of experience in commercial real estate, spanning multifamily investment sales, acquisitions, site development, and capital formation. He began with Marcus & Millichap’s Multifamily Investment Sales Group, then moved to the principal side with Village Green Companies. Since 1999, he has been involved in more than $2 billion in commercial real estate transactions, including communications infrastructure portfolios valued at more than $1 billion at exit.
