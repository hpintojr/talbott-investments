About Talbott Investments

Meet the person behind the investment process.

Our Firm

Talbott Investments acquires and manages value add multifamily communities in growing markets, alongside experienced operating partners. We help busy professionals build passive income through real assets.

Our work centers on three things: disciplined acquisitions, institutional quality operating partners, and clear investor communication.

Leadership

Managing Partner

Stephen Predmore

Stephen founded Talbott Investments after a 30 year career in engineering, where he became a licensed Professional Engineer. A corporate downsizing showed him how exposed a single paycheck can be, and he committed to building wealth through real assets and helping others do the same. Today he builds relationships with investors and operating partners, evaluates acquisitions in target markets, and oversees the investor experience from first call to distribution.
