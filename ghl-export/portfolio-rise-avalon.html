Property Profile

Rise Avalon

A 240 unit value add multifamily community in Charlotte, North Carolina.

Property Overview

Multifamily · Charlotte, North Carolina

Built in 1988 and owned by the same family for about 25 years, the property had never had interior renovations. It was acquired at about a 20% discount to comparable sales, creating a full value add opportunity across every unit.

Why Charlotte

  • An emerging market with strong upside
  • Positive net migration and steady job growth
  • A diversified job base, with no single sector above 20% of the workforce
  • A business and landlord friendly environment

Why This Property

  • A legacy seller who wanted a fast close
  • About a 20% discount to comparable sales
  • A Class B asset in a Class A neighborhood
  • 100% of units can be upgraded
  • Washer and dryer hookups in every unit, for an immediate rent premium

Progress

  • Distributions began within 60 days of closing
  • Exterior renovations completed in 30 days
  • Occupancy increased to 93.1%
  • Average rents increased 3.4%
