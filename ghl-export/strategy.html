How We Invest

Investment Strategy and Approach

How we select markets, underwrite investment opportunities, and oversee every investment.

Investment Focus

Value Add Multifamily, Bought Right

We acquire well located Class B apartment communities that are performing below their potential. These are properties where better management, targeted renovations, and tighter operations can raise income and, with it, value. Our typical hold period is 2 to 5 years.

What we look for

  • Communities of 100 to 300 units, built 1980 to 2000
  • Rents below comparable properties, with room to close the gap
  • Strong locations near jobs, schools, and transportation
  • Owners who have under managed or under invested in the property

How we create value

Better Management

Stronger leasing, collections, and expense control.

Targeted Renovations

Unit interiors and exterior improvements that justify higher rents.

Operational Improvements

Utility billing, amenity fees, and vendor contracts.

Our Process

Underwriting and Approval

Every investment opportunity passes through the same disciplined, three stage process before we commit investor capital. Most never make it past the first stage.

  1. 01

    Market Screening

    We screen every investment opportunity against defined market and property criteria: submarket job and population growth, rent levels relative to comparable properties, the physical condition of the asset, and a clear path to add value. Roughly half of the investment opportunities we see are eliminated at this stage.

  2. 02

    Financial Underwriting

    We build every model from verified numbers, not seller projections: the trailing 12 month financials and rent roll line by line, contractor bids for renovations, and stress tests on every assumption. About half of the remaining investment opportunities are cut here, most for unrealistic seller pricing or insufficient yield.

  3. 03

    Validation and Verification

    The investment opportunities that remain go through on site inspections and assessments of the property’s operations and staff, to confirm the business plan holds up in person. We move forward only when conservative leverage, rate protection, fully funded reserves, and downside returns all meet our standards.

Every investment opportunity is stress tested against

  • Flat rent growth
  • Higher vacancy
  • Rising expenses
  • Higher exit cap rate

How We Protect Capital

Investment Structure and Risk

How our offerings are structured

Talbott partners only with the best operators in each market. We scrutinize every operator’s track record, team, and systems before we commit capital, negotiate favorable terms on behalf of our investors, and then create a dedicated investment fund for each investment opportunity. Investors participate as limited partners, with Talbott as the sponsor.

Investors are paid first

Our structures include a preferred return, paid to investors before Talbott or the operating partner share in any profits. Distributions are paid quarterly. Profits above the preferred return are split between investors and the sponsor according to the terms of each offering. Our compensation depends on investors doing well first.
