The Due Diligence Framework

How to Assess Risk, Operator Quality, and Deal Fit

Replay video coming soon

Key Takeaways

  • A projected return is only as reliable as the assumptions and risks behind it.
  • The operator deserves its own underwriting: track record, alignment, reporting, and how problems get handled.
  • Deal fit matters as much as deal quality. Liquidity, time horizon, and portfolio role have to match your goals.
  • A repeatable diligence process gives you the confidence to pass on deals that look good but are wrong for you.
